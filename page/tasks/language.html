Sample Cases

Representative examples from this task.

Case #1 — Structured Format

Difficulty: Easy
Problem
Write a haiku (5-7-5 syllable structure) about machine learning. The haiku must contain the word "gradient" and not use any word longer than 10 characters.
Example Solution
Gradient flows down
Through layers of the network
Learning to see truth
Reward Function
syllable_check([5,7,5]) AND contains("gradient") AND max_word_len <= 10 → reward = satisfied / total_constraints

Case #2 — Multi-constraint Paragraph

Difficulty: Medium
Problem
Write a 3-sentence product description for a coffee maker. Constraints: (1) Exactly 3 sentences. (2) Total word count between 40-60. (3) Must include the words "brew", "morning", and "aroma". (4) No sentence may exceed 25 words. (5) Must end with a question.
Example Solution
Start every morning with the rich aroma of freshly ground beans, crafted to perfection in minutes. Our precision brew system extracts maximum flavor while keeping your countertop clutter-free and elegant. Ready to transform your daily coffee ritual into something extraordinary?
Reward Function
sentence_count(3) AND word_count(40,60) AND contains_all(["brew","morning","aroma"]) AND max_sentence_words(25) AND ends_with("?") → reward = passed / 5
