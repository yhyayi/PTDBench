Design Principles

Every dataset in DataCall is held to strict standards to ensure reproducibility and fairness.

◇

Verifiable Rewards

All tasks include programmatic reward functions that can be automatically computed without human annotation.

△

Diverse Difficulty

Tasks span multiple difficulty levels, enabling curriculum learning and progressive skill acquisition.

○

Reproducible Evaluation

Standardized evaluation pipelines with fixed seeds, deterministic environments, and published baselines.

□

Open & Extensible

Apache-2.0 licensed. Community contributions welcome. Modular design for easy integration.

Timeline

Key milestones for the DataCall initiative.

September 2026

Data Call Opens

Public submission portal launches. Community can contribute task proposals and datasets.

October 2026

Review & Curation

Submitted data undergoes quality review, deduplication, and reward function validation.

November 2026

Benchmark v1.0 Release

First official release of the curated benchmark with evaluation harness and baselines.

December 2026

Competition Launch

Open competition for RL training on the DataCall benchmark with public leaderboard.
